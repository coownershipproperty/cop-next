# Backlink Outreach Email Templates — May 2026

Three template variants tailored to the kind of outlet you're pitching. Each is short (under 160 words), specific (cites real data, not generic claims), and offers something concrete (data access, expert quote, exclusive angle). Personalise the **[bracketed]** fields per outlet before sending.

The targets list lives in `outreach-targets.csv` — pitch the highest-priority outlets first, in batches of 6–8 per week so you have bandwidth for follow-ups.

**Don't BCC. Don't mass-blast. Don't attach files** — link to anchor pieces on the site instead.

Track responses in a simple spreadsheet (column for date sent, response, follow-up sent, outcome). Even a 5–10% positive response rate at this volume should generate 4–6 backlinks from genuinely authoritative outlets, which is what actually matters for the indexing situation that started this whole project.

## What success looks like

- 4–8 quality backlinks within 90 days from outlets with domain authority above 60.
- 1–2 pieces of substantive coverage that quote the Owner Survey 2026 data.
- Increased indexed-pages count in Google Search Console as the new backlinks signal authority.

## What to avoid

- Pitching the same anchor piece to multiple outlets simultaneously without exclusivity — destroys credibility.
- Paying for placements / sponsored posts (Google penalises these and they don't compound).
- Mass-mailing tools (these get picked up by editor spam filters; cold replies need real-person warmth).
- Following up more than once. After two contacts with no reply, move on.
