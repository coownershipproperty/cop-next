The short answer: Five factors drive resale velocity. (1) Destination demand — premium European Mediterranean and US mountain destinations clear faster than emerging or off-cycle markets. (2) Operator pipeline depth — operators with active buyer waitlists match shares to buyers immediately; operators without that pipeline rely on slower open-market discovery. (3) Listing price discipline — shares priced at the operator's fair-market valuation clear in weeks; shares priced 10%+ above market can sit for months. (4) Property condition — well-maintained homes hold value and clear faster. (5) Broader category sentiment — strong fractional demand environments lift all operators' velocity.

The five velocity drivers ranked

1. Destination demand (largest single driver)

Shares in premium European Mediterranean destinations (Mallorca, Côte d'Azur, Lake Como, Ibiza) and established US mountain resorts (Aspen, Tahoe, Park City) consistently clear faster than shares in emerging or off-cycle destinations. Buyer pipelines in these markets are deeper and more active. Average days-to-resale: 60-120 days vs 180-365 days for less-demanded destinations.

2. Operator pipeline depth

Two operators in the same destination can have very different resale velocity if their buyer-pipeline depths differ. Operators with hundreds of qualified buyers on their waitlist for that destination can match shares immediately; operators with thinner pipelines rely on slower open-market discovery. This factor can dwarf destination effects within a single market.

3. Listing price discipline

Owners who follow operator valuation guidance and price at fair market clear in weeks. Owners who insist on listing 10-20% above operator valuation can sit for months while the market doesn't validate the premium. The most common reason individual shares sit unsold isn't structural — it's pricing.

4. Property condition

Well-maintained homes (active reserve-fund refresh cycles, recent paintwork, fresh furnishings, working appliances) compete strongly. Under-invested properties (tired furniture, deferred maintenance, dated interiors) struggle even with fair pricing. The property's specific condition can swing resale time by 30-50% in either direction relative to its destination's average.

5. Broader category sentiment

In strong fractional demand environments (like 2023-2025 in Europe), all operators see faster resale velocity. In softer environments, all operators see slower velocity. This is the lowest-leverage factor for individual decisions (it affects everything roughly equally) but worth knowing.

Combining the factors

The fastest-clearing shares are typically: well-priced shares in well-maintained properties at premium destinations through operators with deep buyer pipelines. The slowest-clearing shares are typically: over-priced shares in under-invested properties at emerging destinations through operators with thin pipelines.

For sellers, the variables they can control are price discipline (follow operator valuation) and property condition (vote for reserve-fund refresh cycles). The variables they can't control are destination demand (decided at purchase) and operator pipeline depth (decided at purchase).

What this means for buyers

Before purchasing, evaluate the eventual resale picture: is this destination likely to have strong demand in 5-10 years? Does this operator have a deep buyer pipeline that should continue working? Is the property's condition currently strong, with reserve-fund discipline supporting future condition? These three questions matter as much as the share's current price.

What sellers should do to accelerate resale

Three practical actions. First, follow operator valuation guidance on listing price — testing premium pricing usually costs more in carrying time than it gains in price achieved. Second, vote in favour of reasonable reserve-fund-funded refresh cycles during ownership — property condition compounds. Three, list during stronger market conditions when possible — broader category sentiment matters even if not the largest factor.
