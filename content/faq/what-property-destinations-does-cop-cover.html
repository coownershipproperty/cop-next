Destinations we don't currently cover

Three categories of destinations where COP doesn't currently have meaningful inventory. First, emerging markets without established single-property co-ownership operators (Bali, Greek Islands beyond limited inventory). Second, destinations where the fractional market is currently too thin to justify dedicated coverage. Third, destinations where the local regulatory environment makes the LLC structure complicated (some Eastern European destinations).

We track these markets and add coverage as inventory and operator quality justify it.

How destinations get added

Three drivers. First, when an existing partner operator expands inventory into a new destination, that destination joins our marketplace automatically. Second, when buyer demand for a destination crosses a threshold (consistent enquiries with no inventory match), we actively seek operators in that market. Third, when a new operator joins our partner network bringing their existing inventory along.

Why this destination set

The destinations COP covers reflect where single-property co-ownership inventory genuinely exists. We don't list destinations where no qualifying operator inventory is available — that would mislead buyers. The category is concentrated in established luxury second-home markets where institutional-quality operators have built fractional structures over the past 5-15 years. The destinations we cover represent broadly the global single-property co-ownership market.

What's coming next

Three directional trends we're tracking. First, Italian inventory expansion (Tuscany and Lake Como specifically) as European operators scale into Italy. Second, US East Coast expansion (Florida, Hamptons) as US fractional broadens beyond mountain resorts. Third, the gradual addition of more Portuguese inventory beyond the Algarve as that market matures.

How to find inventory for a specific destination

The COP marketplace includes a destination filter that returns current inventory for any destination we cover. If your desired destination isn't on the marketplace, contact us — we can flag whether it's a coverage gap we're working on or a destination where no qualifying operator inventory currently exists.
