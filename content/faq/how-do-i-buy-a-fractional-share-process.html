The short answer: The seven-step fractional purchase process. (1) Identify the property and operator through marketplace research and property viewings. (2) Sign reservation agreement with deposit (typically €5k-€20k). (3) Use the cooling-off period (7-14 days in most EU jurisdictions) for legal and tax review with specialists. (4) Complete KYC and apply for any foreign tax IDs (NIE for Spain, etc.). (5) Sign final share-purchase agreement after legal review. (6) Pay balance via international wire to escrow; LLC member register updated. (7) Onboarding call, booking platform access, first stay scheduled. Cash buyers typically complete in 4-8 weeks; financed purchases add 4-8 weeks for mortgage underwriting.

The seven steps in detail

Step 1 — Identify property and operator

Use the marketplace research period to identify properties matching destination, price, share size and operator preferences. Visit properties in person where possible — a pre-purchase viewing reveals what photos can't. Compare across operators within your destination of choice. Most buyers spend 1-6 months in this exploration phase before committing.

Step 2 — Reservation agreement

Once committed to a specific property, sign the operator's reservation agreement and pay the reservation deposit (typically €5,000-€20,000). The deposit holds the share off the market while you complete due diligence. Cooling-off provisions typically allow full deposit refund if you withdraw within the defined window.

Step 3 — Cooling-off and due diligence

The 7-14 day cooling-off period (in most EU jurisdictions) is your window for legal and tax review. Engage your own cross-border specialist lawyer to review the LLC operating agreement and share-purchase agreement. Engage a cross-border tax specialist to confirm the home-country position. Verify the operator's claims (resale history, reserve fund, etc.).

Step 4 — KYC and foreign tax IDs

Complete buyer KYC (passport, proof of address, source-of-funds documentation) with the operator. Apply for any required foreign tax identification numbers — Spanish NIE if buying a Spanish share; codice fiscale for Italian shares; equivalent for other jurisdictions. Allow 4-8 weeks for foreign tax ID issuance.

Step 5 — Final share-purchase agreement

After legal review and KYC completion, sign the final share-purchase agreement (SPA). The SPA incorporates the LLC operating agreement and finalises the share transfer terms. Have your lawyer review the final version before signing.

Step 6 — Payment and member-register update

Wire the balance to the operator's escrow account or designated notary. Funds are held until completion conditions are met (typically same-day to 5-day window). Upon completion, the LLC's membership register is updated to reflect your ownership; you receive the share certificate / member confirmation.

Step 7 — Onboarding

Operator's owner-services team conducts an onboarding call (typically within 30 days). Booking platform access is activated. First-rotation calendar is shared. First stay can typically be booked within 2-4 months of completion. See first-year experience for what to expect after onboarding.

What can go wrong

Three common bottlenecks. First, NIE issuance for Spanish purchases — can take 6-8 weeks from a busy consulate. Apply early. Second, KYC documentation — some buyers underestimate the source-of-funds paperwork. Gather early. Third, financing — mortgage underwriting can extend the timeline significantly. Cash purchases consistently complete faster.

What buyers should do at each step

Critical actions. Step 1: visit multiple properties before committing. Step 2: read the reservation agreement carefully before signing. Step 3: use the cooling-off — engage your own lawyer and tax specialist. Step 4: gather KYC documents proactively. Step 5: read the final SPA carefully; don't sign under pressure. Step 6: confirm wire-transfer mechanics with your bank in advance. Step 7: attend the onboarding call to understand the booking platform fully.
