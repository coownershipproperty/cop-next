# German (DE) translation conventions for destination pillars

Reference brand: **MYNE Homes** (myne-homes.com). MYNE is the German market leader for
fractional luxury second homes and the closest direct comparator to COP for German-speaking
buyers. Their wording is the benchmark for tone and terminology.

This file also draws on our existing DE pages (`pages/de/`, `messages/de.json`) for
consistency with the rest of our German corpus.

---

## Form of address

**"Sie" (formal)** throughout — never "du". MYNE uses Sie, all luxury German real estate
brands use Sie, and our existing DE pages use Sie. Switching to "du" is fine for casual
brands but feels jarring for a luxury second-home audience.

Examples:
- ✅ "Ihr Anteil ist in einer LLC gehalten" — your share is held in an LLC
- ✅ "Sie erhalten ungefähr 45 Tage persönlicher Nutzung pro Jahr"
- ❌ "Dein Anteil ist..." (avoid)
- ❌ "Du erhältst..." (avoid)

Possessives: "Ihr Anteil", "Ihre Immobilie", "Ihr Aufenthalt" (capital I — formal).

## Real estate terminology

| EN | DE preferred | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| property / second home | **Immobilie** / **Ferienimmobilie** / **Zweitwohnsitz** | MYNE uses "Immobilien" everywhere. "Ferienimmobilie" = vacation home. "Zweitwohnsitz" = second residence (more legal/administrative tone). |
| co-ownership / fractional ownership | **Miteigentum** / **Bruchteilseigentum** | MYNE uses **Miteigentum** as the headline term. "Bruchteilseigentum" is the strict legal term but reads more dry. Use Miteigentum in body copy, Bruchteilseigentum once when introducing the legal concept. |
| share (1/8 share) | **Anteil** | "1/8-Anteil" or "Achtelanteil". MYNE writes "1/8 Anteil". |
| owner / co-owner | **Miteigentümer** / **Eigentümer** | "Miteigentümer" for fractional owners specifically, "Eigentümer" for owners generally. |
| villa | **Villa** | unchanged |
| chalet | **Chalet** | unchanged |
| apartment | **Apartment** / **Wohnung** | "Apartment" for the modern/luxury feel, "Wohnung" for the standard residential meaning |
| holiday home / vacation home | **Ferienimmobilie** / **Ferienhaus** | "Ferienhaus" is more colloquial; "Ferienimmobilie" matches MYNE's tone |
| LLC | **LLC** | keep as-is. Spell out as **Limited Liability Company** on first mention if introducing the concept. NEVER use a German equivalent (GbR, KG, GmbH) — they are different legal vehicles |
| land registry | **Grundbuch** | the German land registry — for our pages just say "im jeweiligen Grundbuch eingetragen" if referring to the country's land register generically |

| resale | **Wiederverkauf** | the standard term |

## Co-ownership-specific phrasings

- **"deeded 1/8 share of a luxury second home"** → "ein eingetragener 1/8-Anteil an einer Luxus-Ferienimmobilie"
- **"held in a purpose-built LLC alongside seven other co-owners"** → "gehalten in einer eigens dafür gegründeten LLC gemeinsam mit sieben weiteren Miteigentümern"
- **"approximately 45 days of personal use per year"** → "rund 45 Tage persönliche Nutzung pro Jahr"
- **"fair-rotation calendar"** → "fair rotierender Belegungskalender" or "fair geregelter Buchungskalender"
- **"professional management team"** → "professionelles Verwaltungsteam"
- **"real, recorded property equity"** → "echtes, eingetragenes Eigentumsrecht an Immobilien"
- **"not a timeshare, not a holiday club"** → "kein Timeshare, kein Holiday Club"
- **"fully managed"** → "vollständig verwaltet" or "Rundum-Verwaltung"
- **"6–7 weeks of personal use a year"** → "6 bis 7 Wochen persönlicher Nutzung pro Jahr"
- **"single international portfolio relationship"** → "eine einzige internationale Portfolio-Beziehung"
- **"~1 month on average"** → "im Durchschnitt rund einen Monat"

## Numbers, currency, units

- **Decimal separator: comma**, thousands separator: period (or thin space)
  - `€1.500.000` or `1 500 000 €` (EUR symbol after with thin space is most German)
  - `€1,5 Mio.` (1.5 million euros)
  - "1.500 €" (1,500 euros)
- **Temperatures**: keep `°C (°F)` pairing — Germans expect Celsius; °F is the international cross-reference
- **Distances**: kilometres only — DON'T add miles in parens
- **Time**: 24-hour clock for opening times if used
- **1/8**: write as "1/8" (matches MYNE's "1/8 Anteil")

## Tone and stylistic notes

1. **Compound nouns are your friend** — German naturally builds long compound nouns. Embrace them: "Luxusferienimmobilie", "Bruchteilseigentumsstruktur", "Verwaltungsteammitarbeiter". Don't over-do it though — break apart with hyphens if a compound exceeds about 25 letters: "Bruchteilseigentums-Vertrag" reads better than "Bruchteilseigentumsvertrag".

2. **Sentence rhythm**: German tolerates longer sentences than English. Don't artificially shorten — keep the EN paragraph structure but build sentences with subordinate clauses naturally (das, dessen, in dem, woraus). MYNE's tone is matter-of-fact and informative, not breathless.

3. **Adjective endings**: be careful with der/die/das endings. "Luxusvilla" → die Villa → "die exklusive Villa", "Anteil" → der → "der eingetragene Anteil".

4. **Avoid Anglicisms where there's a clean German word** but DO keep:
   - "LLC" (legal vehicle name)
   - "Timeshare" (compares directly to English)
   - "Concierge" (used in luxury hospitality)
   - "Lifestyle" (universally used)
   - "Co-Working" / "Workation" (modern usage)

5. **Comma rules**: German uses Oxford-comma-equivalent before "und" only sparingly; don't force it. Always comma before subordinate clauses introduced by "dass, weil, ob, wenn, obwohl, während".

6. **Capitalisation**: ALL nouns capitalised. "Die Immobilie", "der Anteil", "das Eigentum" — easy to forget mid-sentence.

7. **MYNE's voice**: confident, founder-led, slightly aspirational but never breathless. They favour concrete benefits over emotional copy. Match this tone.

## Banned vocabulary / phrasings

- **Avoid "Eigentumswohnung"** — that means "freehold apartment" (singular ownership), confusing in fractional context
- **Avoid "Ferienclub" / "Ferienanlage"** when describing the LLC structure — sounds like timeshare
- **Avoid "Anlage" alone** for "investment" — say "Kapitalanlage" or "Vermögensanlage" if needed
- **Avoid "Eigentümergemeinschaft" (WEG)** — that's the specific German condominium-association concept, different from our LLC
- **Avoid translating "fractional ownership" as "Teileigentum"** — Teileigentum has a specific German legal meaning (commercial unit ownership in a mixed-use building). Use **Miteigentum** instead.

## Country tax / legal terms (keep in original where appropriate)

- **IMI / AIMI** (Portugal) → keep as-is, gloss in German on first mention: "die IMI (Imposto Municipal sobre Imóveis — die kommunale Grundsteuer)"
- **IMU / TARI** (Italy) → keep as-is, gloss: "die IMU (kommunale Immobiliensteuer)"
- **IBI** (Spain) → keep as-is, gloss: "die IBI (Impuesto sobre Bienes Inmuebles — die kommunale Grundsteuer)"
- **FIRPTA** (USA) → keep as-is, gloss: "die FIRPTA-Vorschriften (Foreign Investment in Real Property Tax Act)"
- **Conservatória do Registo Predial** / **Catasto** / **Catastro** / **county recorder** → keep original term, gloss as "(das jeweilige Grundbuch)" or "(die zuständige Liegenschaftsbehörde)"
- **réserve héréditaire / legítima / legittima** → keep, gloss as "(Pflichtteilsrecht)"
