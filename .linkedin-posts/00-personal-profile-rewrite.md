# Personal LinkedIn profile rewrite — Dylan Olsson

**IMPORTANT positioning rule** — COP is an agent / marketplace. We never name operator partners (the firms whose listings we curate) anywhere public. Reason: a client who knows the partner name will go direct and cut us out. The website doesn't name them, and the LinkedIn presence must match.

Three sections to update, in order of impact:

---

## 1. Headline (priority #1 — this shows in every search result, every comment, every DM thread)

**Current** (probably): something like "Co-founder at X" or default

**New** (220-char limit, target ~180):

> Founder at COP — the curated marketplace for fractional luxury homes across Europe & the US · 1/8 deeded ownership, not timeshare · 350+ properties

**Why it works:**
- "Founder at COP" — clear role, easy to grok
- "curated marketplace" — editorial positioning, not pushy sales
- "Europe & the US" — international scope
- "1/8 deeded ownership, not timeshare" — removes the #1 objection in the first impression
- "350+ properties" — credibility number

**Where to update:** Profile → Pencil icon on top intro card → Edit intro → Headline field.

---

## 2. About section (priority #2 — anyone who clicks through reads this)

**Target length:** ~1,100 chars (LinkedIn shows the first ~300 before "see more", everything else has to earn the click). Open with the hook.

> Franco-Argentinian, raised in London, based in Marbella. I run COP — the curated marketplace for prime second homes across Europe and the US, owned as 1/8 deeded shares.
>
> Here's the thesis: almost no one actually uses a second home more than 6 weeks a year. Owning the other 46 weeks is a tax on your time and your capital. 1/8 fractional ownership — real fee-simple title, not timeshare — solves the maths.
>
> For €150k you can own one-eighth of a Provençal villa in Valbonne. For €515k, one-eighth of a 5-bed Mallorcan finca with a saltwater pool. For ~$1M, a ski-in/ski-out residence on Snowmass Mountain.
>
> COP is the editorial layer on top of a fragmented market — we hand-pick every listing, verify it, photograph it, and translate it for English, French, Spanish and German buyers. Since 2022 we've grown to 350+ verified homes across Spain, France, Italy, the US and beyond.
>
> Most of our buyers are American, British or Latin American — families wanting a foothold in Europe without the full-time cost of a second home. If that's you, take a look.
>
> 350+ homes live now: https://co-ownership-property.com
>
> Always happy to chat in EN / FR / ES. DMs open.

**Where to update:** Profile → About section → pencil icon.
