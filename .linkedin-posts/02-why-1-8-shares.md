## Hook (first line is everything — this is what shows before "see more")

> 1/8 ownership is not timeshare. It's the model that owns the home you actually want.

## Body (1,180 chars)

> **Timeshare:** you buy a *week*. You don't own anything. You can't sell it for what you paid.
>
> **Fractional 1/8:** you buy *one-eighth of the property itself*. Your name is on the deed. You can sell your share whenever you want — at market price.
>
> Here's the maths on a €1.2M villa most people would otherwise leave empty 320 days a year:
>
> ▸ €150,000 — your share
> ▸ 45 nights per year — your usage
> ▸ Maintenance, taxes, pool, gardener — split eight ways
> ▸ Furnished, professionally managed, ready to walk in
> ▸ List your share whenever you want, at whatever price you want
>
> The 8-owner LLC structure is identical to how families have co-owned second homes for generations. The difference: you don't need a family. The home is curated, the LLC is set up for you, and the management is professional.
>
> Why does this work?
>
> Because almost no one actually uses a second home more than 6 weeks a year. Owning the other 46 weeks is a tax on your time and your capital.
>
