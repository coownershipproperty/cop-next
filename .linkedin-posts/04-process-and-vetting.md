# Post 4 — "How we vet every property" (post from COP page, day 10)

**Goal:** address the obvious objection — *who's actually behind this, and how do you know the homes are real?* This post explains COP's curation process and builds credibility through transparency, **without** naming the operator partners (clients who learn the partner names will go direct and cut us out).

**Format:** Plain text. The process is the content.

**Best time:** Tuesday or Wednesday morning. Builds on the week-1 conversation.

---

## Hook

> "Yes, but who is actually behind these homes?" is the most common question we get.

## Body (~1,180 chars)

> COP isn't a developer. We don't build, sell or operate the homes ourselves. We're a curated marketplace — the editorial layer on top of a fragmented market.
>
> Here's how the process works:
>
> ▸ Every home is held in its own LLC. You're on the deed of that LLC for one-eighth. Real fee-simple title. The structure is identical to how families have co-owned second homes for generations — but with eight strangers instead of cousins, and professional management instead of family arguments.
>
> ▸ Every share is freely transferable. Sell to anyone, at any price, at any time.
>
> ▸ Every home is operationally run — housekeeping, calendaring, dispute resolution, maintenance, taxes, utilities — by a licensed manager. Owners arrive, walk in, walk out. No keys, no bills, no surprises.
>
> ▸ Every listing on COP has been visited (where possible) or independently verified. We photograph, translate and describe each one ourselves. Honesty over hype — we don't list a home if the maths doesn't work for an eighth-owner.
>
> What we don't do: chase yield, sell timeshare weeks, or take commission from the operator. Our job is to be the buyer's editor.
>
> 350+ homes curated → https://co-ownership-property.com
>
