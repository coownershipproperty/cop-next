# MYNE Homes — How They Actually Drive Sales

Research compiled May 2026 from public sources. Where evidence is weak or absent, I say so rather than guess.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

MYNE Homes did not get to 400+ shares sold across ~130 properties by accident, and David's hunch that "they don't run ads" is almost certainly wrong on the small claims but right on the big one: **the dominant channel isn't paid digital — it's an industrial-scale earned-media + founder-PR + content engine, layered on top of a serious in-house sales team and a property-fair circuit.**

The three biggest channels, in rough order of contribution to brand and pipeline:

1. **PR / earned media at extraordinary volume.** MYNE has run a multi-year, multi-country PR campaign that has placed the company in essentially every German business publication (Handelsblatt, Business Insider Gründerszene, Tagesspiegel, Capital, NTV, ARD, Galileo, RTL7, TV Berlin) and an unusually deep UK tabloid sweep (The Times, Daily Mail, Daily Express, The Independent, Daily Star, This Is Money, Property Industry Eye, Yahoo News). Their `/press` page lists 16+ broadcast TV/streaming features and 11+ podcast appearances by founder Nikolaus Thomale alone.
2. **A real in-house sales team, not a fintech-style self-serve funnel.** LinkedIn lists MYNE at ~96 employees (one source) with ~43–50 open positions worldwide and 11–17 in Berlin, including Sales Operations Manager, Senior Sales Manager, Junior Acquisition Manager, Regional Manager Deutschland, Sales Assistant, After-Sales Manager. The listings page itself is gated: you cannot see a property without giving name/email/phone/country — i.e. every single listing is a lead-capture form, and a human sales rep follows up.
3. **Founder-led content + a German-language SEO/blog engine + physical real-estate fairs.** Thomale appears on 10+ German business/finance/proptech podcasts. They have a deep `/blog` with 20+ co-ownership/timeshare/financing comparison articles built for SEO. They actively exhibit at the Second Home Expo circuit (Utrecht, Maastricht, Ghent, Antwerp — they confirmed attending Utrecht 2025 in a LinkedIn post).

Paid social/Google appears to be a supporting tactic, not the engine — but it does exist (their Facebook page hosts video-style price ads like "1/8-Eigentum in Frankreich für 139.000 €"). The real moat is the PR + sales-team combo, which COP cannot easily copy with two people.

## 2. Paid Advertising

**Honest answer: limited direct evidence of large paid-media spend, but they do run paid social and have run airline-magazine print.**

- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram).** MYNE has an active Facebook page (`facebook.com/mynehomes`) categorised as "Real Estate Investment Firm". The page hosts product-style video posts that look like ad creatives — e.g. "1/8-Eigentum in Frankreich für 139.000 €" with a CTA to "register for access to exclusive exposés" — strongly suggesting active Meta paid social. I could not pull Meta Ad Library results directly (Meta blocks unauthenticated scraping and Google does not index the library well), so I cannot confirm scale or country footprint, only that the creative format is consistent with paid distribution.
- **Instagram.** 34K followers on `@mynehomes` (well above organic-only growth for a 4-year-old B2C real estate brand — usually indicates promoted posts/Reels).
- **LinkedIn.** Active company page with 14,262 followers, frequent organic posts (e.g. their May 2026 post about Second Home Expo Utrecht). No public evidence of LinkedIn paid ads, but as a B2C luxury brand they probably don't lean on LinkedIn paid.
- **Google Ads.** No public evidence of large branded or non-branded Google Ads spend. Their blog is heavily SEO-engineered (Co-Ownership vs. Timeshare, "How to get out of a timeshare", "Financing a holiday property", etc.) which is consistent with an SEO-first rather than SEM-first strategy.
- **Print / out-of-home.** One secondary source mentions ads appearing in **Eurowings in-flight magazine**. I could not directly verify this with a primary source.
- **No evidence** of TV commercials (unlike Pacaso, which runs broadcast TV in the US/UK/DE/SE).

**Bottom line: paid is real but not their primary firepower.** The PR + content + sales team gets disproportionate weight in their mix.

## 5. Marketing Playbook

**Content & SEO:**
- `/blog` has ~20+ long-form articles, heavily oriented to comparison/decision-stage keywords: "Co-Ownership vs. Timeshare", "How to get out of a timeshare", "Different models for owning a holiday home", "Pros and Cons of Co-Ownership", "An alternative to financing your holiday property", "Right time to buy a holiday property", etc. Mix of original explainers and customer-story posts ("Co-ownership experience in Mallorca", "Co-ownership experience in Austria").
- They appear to be intentionally hijacking timeshare-related search intent — a high-volume, high-intent keyword bucket where US incumbents don't compete in German.

**Brand & social:**
- Instagram `@mynehomes` — 34K followers, Reels and aspirational property content.
- Facebook — active page, product-style video posts.
- LinkedIn — 14K followers, executive thought-leadership cadence (e.g. Second Home Expo posts).
- YouTube — channel exists (`UCkckZjDQWmRgxEGyNDtC1ew`); content is testimonial-led ("Miteigentümerin berichtet über Ihre Erfahrungen mit MYNE-Miteigentum im Chalet in Österreich") plus an explainer hero video. View counts are not impressive — YouTube is not a primary acquisition channel for them.

**Events:**
- **Second Home Expo** circuit confirmed — their May 2026 LinkedIn post described "Great energy at Second Home Expo Utrecht! With over 10,000 attendees, it was a fantastic few days connecting with both new faces and familiar ones." This expo runs 6 editions/year in Utrecht, Maastricht, Ghent, Antwerp. That's a Benelux-targeted real-estate-fair strategy aligned with their stated Benelux expansion priority (per Silicon Canals interview with Thomale).
- No evidence of EXPO REAL Munich participation.

**Partnerships:**
- **VillaCircle acquisition (2023)** — bought their main German competitor for an undisclosed seven-figure sum; customer base + Austrian properties absorbed. This was a buy-vs-build move for inventory + leads.
- **No public broker / "refer a buyer" program** found. Unlike Vivla (which signed deals with Engel & Völkers, John Taylor, Remax Collection, Lantana Premium), MYNE does not appear to use traditional real-estate-agent referral channels — they prefer direct demand generation.

**Brochure / lead magnet:**
- Main top-of-funnel CTA across the site is "Request concept brochure" — classic high-intent lead capture for a considered B2C purchase. Gated brochure → email → sales call sequence.

## 6. Funnel Mechanics

**Gating:**
- `/listings` is gated. Visitors cannot browse properties without submitting: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Country (180-country dropdown), and accepting being contacted by phone/email. There is no "guest browse" mode. **Every prospective buyer becomes a marketing-qualified lead from minute one.**
- The "Request concept brochure" CTA is repeated 5+ times on the about page alone.

**Booking tools on site:**
- Direct evidence in their Next.js source: **Calendly is preloaded** (`<script id="calendly-script" src="https://assets.calendly.com/...">`) on every page. Sales reps almost certainly send a Calendly link after first contact, allowing self-service appointment booking.
- **Trustpilot widget is preloaded** site-wide (4.7-star rating shown on listings page) — they treat third-party social proof as a conversion lever.

**Social proof on listings:**
- Live testimonial carousel on `/listings` (Julian S. Mallorca, Thomas B. Österreich, Ute F. Österreich, Constanze R., Michael D., Dorian H., Esther R., Markus L., Alfred G., Family Feig) — names + locations + verbatim quotes, several explicitly labelled "*formerly VillaCircle" for continuity.
- I could not confirm an "X of 8 shares sold" live counter on individual property pages (it's behind the registration gate). MYNE's general property data does expose a status field (`propertyStatus`: available / in-development / renovation / sold) per the COP scraper notes in CLAUDE.md, but it's not clear they display a live sold-shares count.

**Response speed:** I did not submit a test enquiry. From job descriptions and the multi-layered sales-team structure (SDR → senior sales → after-sales), the implied SLA is rapid human follow-up — almost certainly within hours, possibly minutes during business hours.

**3rd-party portal presence:**
- **Kocomo** (a fractional-ownership aggregator/marketplace at `kocomo.com/providers/myne`) cross-lists MYNE inventory.
- I found **no evidence** of MYNE inventory on ImmobilienScout24, Immowelt, or Engel & Völkers — they appear to keep distribution proprietary, which fits the gated-listings logic.

## 9. Implications for COP — The Five Highest-Leverage Borrows

You're not going to match a 70-person sales team with two people, so don't try. But the structural choices MYNE made are copyable:

### 1. Stop being a browse-able directory; become a gated lead-capture funnel
Every MYNE property view is a captured lead. COP currently lets visitors see everything for free and only converts via the "Exclusive Photos" email gate. **Move the listings index behind a single soft gate** (name + email + country) and you'll multiply your captured-lead volume 5–10x without losing organic SEO (the property pages can remain crawlable; the "see prices / shares available / photos" UI is what gates). This is the single biggest leverage point.

### 2. Run an aggressive UK-tabloid + German-business PR sprint
MYNE's UK strategy is replicable on a £3–5K/month retainer with a small consumer-PR agency. The trick is the angle library: each press hit is a different "story arc" with one MYNE research stat. COP could commission one piece of survey data ("70% of Brits would buy a holiday home if they could split the cost") and turn it into 8–10 placements across Daily Mail / Express / Times / Independent / This Is Money in a single quarter. **You don't need to publish the survey — you just need a number.**

### 3. Run the founder-podcast circuit relentlessly
Thomale appears on 10+ podcasts. Each appearance is a 45-minute brand-equity infusion that costs zero. David should be on Property Hub UK, PropertyCEO, The Property Podcast, Sifted's podcast, EU-Startups Podcast, FinanceFwd, and every "expat in Spain"/"second home buyer" podcast. **Block 1 day/month for podcast outreach + 1 day/month for recording.** Use a podcast booking agency if needed (~£1K/month, several exist).

### 4. Build the SEO/blog stack around timeshare and "second home" comparison intent
MYNE's blog explicitly hijacks "co-ownership vs. timeshare", "how to get out of a timeshare", "different models for owning a holiday home" — high-intent, low-competition keywords. COP has zero blog presence on the same terms in English. **Twenty high-quality comparison articles in 6 months would outrank MYNE on UK terms** because MYNE's articles are slightly translated-from-German and not optimised for British English search behaviour.

### 5. Get on the property-fair circuit + add a partner program
- **Second Home Expo Utrecht/Antwerp** — €5–10K/event for a booth. 10,000 attendees per show. Same audience demographic. Vivla also exhibits these — COP could too.
- **Partner with UK estate agents** the Vivla way, not the MYNE way. Vivla signed Engel & Völkers, John Taylor, Remax, Lantana. **A 1.5% finder's fee paid to luxury second-home estate agents in Marbella / Mallorca / Sotogrande would unlock distribution MYNE explicitly does NOT have.** This is an "anti-MYNE" wedge — turn MYNE's direct-only model into your distribution advantage.

**What NOT to try:** TV ads (Pacaso's territory), large Meta paid scale (capital-intensive and MYNE has more runway), an in-house 50-person sales team. Those require either Pacaso-scale capital or MYNE-scale capital and you have neither.
