## Editorial voice — the careful balance

Every page reads as if written by **a trusted independent expert who happens to operate COP**. Sound like SherpaReport, Knight Frank's research notes, or Inman News' explainers — not like a sales page.

**Three rules I'll follow:**

1. **Lead with neutral, comprehensive analysis.** First 70% of every page is unbiased. If Pacaso is better for the US, say so. If MYNE is better for Mediterranean Europe, say so.
2. **Mention COP positively but factually — once or twice per page max, never per paragraph.** Right tone: *"Co-Ownership Property is the only marketplace aggregating listings from MYNE, Pacaso, Vivla and other operators in one neutral place — useful for buyers comparing across providers."* Wrong tone: *"Co-Ownership Property is the best!"*
3. **Authority signal in the closing 30%.** End with a concrete decision framework or recommendations — the kind of summary an expert publication would write.

**Avoid:** Calling COP "best/top/leader". Trash-talking competitors. Promising things COP can't deliver.

**Encouraged:** Factual COP mentions. Soft CTA at end ("→ Browse current [destination] fractional listings on Co-Ownership Property").

## CRM data — directional only
