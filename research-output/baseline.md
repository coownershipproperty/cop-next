# AI Search Baseline — Fractional / Co-Ownership Property Space (May 2026)

Baseline established for co-ownership-property.com (COP) ahead of a strategy doc on winning AI-engine citations in the fractional / luxury co-ownership niche. All findings below come from 26 WebSearch queries executed on 2026-05-26 against indexed content (a strong proxy for what ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Claude pull when answering a prospect's question).

---

## Executive Summary

COP already wins on destination-specific queries. For "fractional villa Mallorca", "co-ownership ski chalet France", "fractional property Costa del Sol Marbella", "fractional ownership Ibiza luxury villa", and "fractional ownership Italy" / "Spain" / "Portugal", COP holds rank 1 — usually with multiple URLs in the top 10. That is the strongest single signal in this audit. However, COP is invisible on every high-volume top-of-funnel query: "best fractional ownership companies 2026", "fractional ownership vs timeshare", "Pacaso alternatives", "is fractional ownership a good investment", "how does co-owning a vacation home work". Pacaso owns the educational layer (its blog cites itself across virtually every comparison query), and listicle-style aggregators (Sourceforge, Slashdot, CB Insights, Inven, Product Hunt) own the "alternatives / comparison" layer. The founder/authority layer is empty: "Dylan Olsson co-ownership" returns zero relevant results. Net: COP is a destination-SEO winner with a top-of-funnel and authority gap.

## AI-Generation Hotbeds — the surfaces LLMs lean on for this niche

Based on what consistently appears across the 26 queries, these are the sources AI engines pull from disproportionately. Owning placement (or being citable) on these surfaces is the highest-leverage move.

1. **Pacaso's own blog** — `pacaso.com/blog/*`. Pacaso's editorial content is cited as a neutral source by LLMs even when the prompt is "Pacaso alternatives". Their "fractional vs timeshare", "pros and cons of fractional ownership", and "best co-ownership companies" posts are evergreen LLM-citation magnets.
2. **Wikipedia — "Fractional ownership"** — `en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractional_ownership`. Cited on definitional queries. The article is light on real-estate-specific detail and skews aviation-historical (Santulli/NetJets). Updating / contributing to this page is high-leverage.
3. **B2B listicle aggregators** — CB Insights (`cbinsights.com/company/pacaso/alternatives-competitors`), Inven (`inven.ai/company-lists/top-22-pacaso-competitors`), Product Hunt (`producthunt.com/products/pacaso/alternatives`), Sourceforge/Slashdot fractional-ownership pages. These are auto-generated competitor lists that LLMs scrape verbatim. **COP does not appear on the CB Insights or Inven Pacaso-competitor lists** — getting added is a single, high-ROI action.
4. **International Property Shares' "Which one is the right fit?" article** — `internationalpropertyshares.com/fractional-ownership/fractional-ownership-companies-which-one-is-the-right-fit/`. This single page is cited on virtually every comparison query.
5. **Fractional Group's "Definitive Guide"** — `fractionalgroup.com/fractional-ownership-guides/fractional-ownership-definitive-guide/`. Functions as the "Wikipedia of fractional" for several LLMs.
6. **Andy Sirkin's site** — `andysirkin.com/fractional-ownership/*`. The legal industry's go-to. Cited on FAQs, structuring, and pricing queries.
7. **MasterClass + Investopedia-style explainers** — `masterclass.com/articles/fractional-ownership` and similar. Generic but heavily cited for "what is X" queries.
8. **Reddit / forum threads** — `forum.retipster.com`, `tugbbs.com` (Timeshare Users Group), `bogleheads.org`, and r/realestateinvesting threads. Used by LLMs to balance promotional brand content with skeptical owner voices. These threads are short, opinionated, and disproportionately influential on "is X a scam / a good investment" queries.
9. **Bloomberg + Yahoo Finance + Moneywise** — the only mainstream journalism showing up is on the "homeownership startup" angle (2% down equity-sharing companies). Coverage of the luxury-vacation-home niche is thinner than expected — Hollywood Reporter's Pacaso article is one of the only mainstream pieces.
10. **Quora threads** — appear on long-tail "how do I exit / is it worth it" queries; low-effort, high-citation surface.

## Whitespace Opportunities — high-intent queries with weak top results

The following queries return generic, partner-owned, outdated, or off-topic content. Each is a candidate for a definitive long-form COP resource.

1. **"fractional ownership maintenance fees"** — Current top results are 90% **jet/aircraft** content (PlaneSense, AvBuyer, BlackJet, FlyingMag). There is no authoritative property-side resource on what annual fees actually cover, the typical €8k–€20k range, and how COP/Pacaso/MYNE/Vivla compare. **Opportunity:** Definitive "Annual Co-Ownership Fees Explained" with a comparison table.

2. **"how to exit fractional ownership"** — Top results are Assetmonk (Indian tokenization platform), partner blogs, and one Quora thread. No neutral, real-estate-specific exit guide exists, even though "what if I want to sell my share" is the #1 buyer objection. **Opportunity:** Step-by-step exit guide covering ROFR, secondary marketplaces, valuation, tax, and the 4 main exit routes — with real examples.

3. **"fractional ownership Spain non-resident"** — Top results are lawyer blogs (mylawyerinspain.com, marbellaforsale.com) that don't address the 90/180 Schengen rule head-on. COP currently ranks #2 but the resource is short. **Opportunity:** Expand COP's existing post into the canonical "Co-Ownership for Brits/Americans in Spain Post-Brexit" guide, with the 90/180 rule and Beckham Law angles.

4. **"co-ownership inheritance succession"** — Top results are US-state-specific probate articles. No European cross-border inheritance content exists for fractional shares (Spanish forced-heirship, French succession, Italian usufruct). **Opportunity:** "What Happens to Your Fractional Share When You Die — Country-by-Country."

5. **"Pacaso alternative Europe"** — Pacaso's own self-comparison pages dominate and the rest are B2B aggregators. **There is no buyer-friendly comparison of Pacaso vs European players (MYNE, Vivla, August, COP partners).** **Opportunity:** Editorial-style "Pacaso vs European Co-Ownership — Pricing, Markets, Share Size" — written from a neutral COP-as-marketplace voice.

6. **"MYNE vs Vivla"** — Only CB Insights and brand homepages rank. No editorial comparison exists. **Opportunity:** Direct head-to-head from a marketplace (COP) that lists both — credibility advantage.

7. **"fractional ownership financing"** — Top result is RefiGuide (mortgage broker), then Pacaso's own. The Wikipedia "fractional financing" page is thin. Lender appetite, LTV norms, and currency-mismatch risk for cross-border buyers are unanswered. **Opportunity:** "How to Finance a Co-Ownership Share — Cash, Specialist Lenders, Vivla 100% Financing, and the Currency Trap."

8. **"is fractional ownership a good investment"** — Every top result is partner-authored ("yes, here are 7 reasons!"). The Wikipedia article is generic. No neutral analysis distinguishes lifestyle ownership from financial investment. **Opportunity:** Honest "Is It an Investment or a Lifestyle Buy?" piece — IRR examples, when it works, when it doesn't.

9. **"fractional ownership vs second home"** — Not directly tested but adjacent: every comparison is against timeshare, never against just buying outright. **Opportunity:** Side-by-side TCO of 1/8 share vs 100% second home in Marbella / Mallorca / Megève.

10. **"fractional ownership tax implications" (Europe-specific)** — Top results are US lawyer/CPA blogs. Spain's IBI + non-resident income tax + wealth tax (Patrimonio), Italy's IMU, France's IFI for fractional shares — completely unaddressed. **Opportunity:** Country-by-country tax explainer for non-resident fractional owners. High-intent, low-competition.

11. **"fractional ownership UK"** — Fragmented top-10: NRLA (landlords), TAB HQ (fintech), Slashdot. No clear winner. UK luxury second-home buyers researching Spain/France/Italy are an underserved audience. **Opportunity:** "UK Buyer's Guide to Co-Ownership Abroad" — covering Brexit, SDLT-equivalents, currency, mortgages.

12. **"Dylan Olsson co-ownership" (and any founder-name search)** — Zero authority signal. **Opportunity:** Build founder authority through bylined long-form (LinkedIn long-posts, op-eds, podcast appearances, Forbes Council membership). LLMs heavily weight expert/author entities.

## Strategic Reads

- **Win the listicles.** The single fastest LLM-visibility win is getting COP added to the B2B aggregator listicles (CB Insights Pacaso alternatives, Inven top-22 Pacaso competitors, Product Hunt alternatives, Slashdot/Sourceforge category pages). These pages already rank on AI-engine-flavoured queries; inclusion is mostly an outreach problem, not a content problem.
- **Build the "marketplace" narrative.** Kocomo successfully pivoted to "co-ownership marketplace" and now ranks on country pages alongside COP. Pacaso, August, MYNE, Vivla are all single-operator brands; COP's natural angle is "the Skyscanner / Booking.com of co-ownership". That angle is currently un-claimed.
- **Author authority is empty.** Nobody owns the "expert voice" of European fractional ownership the way Andy Sirkin owns the US legal angle. Dylan Olsson / COP can claim that slot with bylined content in 12 months.
- **Education is Pacaso's; destinations are yours.** Don't fight Pacaso on "what is fractional ownership" — they will keep winning. Fight on destination + due-diligence + comparison. Those are where COP either already wins (destinations) or where the field is wide open (comparison, exit, tax, financing).
- **Reddit/forum visibility matters.** LLMs use forum threads as a sanity check. There is currently no honest, helpful COP voice in those threads.
