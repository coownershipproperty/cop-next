# Terminology audit — Pacaso vs Sojourn8 vs MYNE
**Date:** 2 June 2026
**Purpose:** Decide whether COP should split into co-ownership vs fractional-ownership pages, by looking at what the established players actually do.

## TL;DR

All three competitors **lead with "co-ownership"** and use **"fractional ownership" as a 1-mention SEO synonym**. Pacaso — a US company — chose co-ownership over fractional ownership. None of them maintain duplicate pages.

What does change by region is the **noun for the home itself**: US says "vacation home", UK/EU says "holiday home" or "second home".

That's a much cheaper edit than duplicating destination/FAQ pages.

Their domain is literally `pacaso.com` and brand-search dominates — but content choice is unambiguous.

They've made the choice COP is considering — and they did it on a *single page set*, not duplicates.

## Recommendation for COP

**Do NOT create duplicate sets of pages.** Three reasons:

1. **No competitor does it.** Even Pacaso, in the US, runs on "co-ownership" alone. Sojourn8 and MYNE — both with multi-country audiences just like COP — solve this with synonym mentions on a single page set.
2. **Google would treat duplicates as doorway pages.** The risk vs reward is bad.
3. **Your domain is `co-ownership-property.com`.** It's a brand asset for that exact term. Pacaso owns "co-ownership" in the US partly because their content commits to it. You can do the same.

**Do INSTEAD — three small content sweeps, all on the same URLs:**

### A. Add the Rosetta-stone sentence on every key page (high impact, low effort)
Borrow MYNE's pattern. On `/how-it-works/`, `/about-us/`, all destination pages and FAQs, add one line in the first paragraph:
> "Co-ownership — also known as **fractional ownership** in the US, or **second home co-ownership** more broadly — gives you deeded title to a share of the property."

This single edit catches the "fractional ownership" SEO term without splitting page authority.

### B. Add hreflang `en-US` variants pointing at the existing pages (one-time tech change)
Tell Google "these pages serve both UK and US audiences." Pacaso doesn't even bother — they just rank because they're the brand — but for a newer site this signals geo-intent without duplicating content.

### C. Locale-aware noun: "holiday home" vs "vacation home"
- Today COP mostly uses "holiday home" (UK English). 
- For US-coded queries you'd ideally swap to "vacation home." 
- Cheapest version: add "vacation home" as an alternate term in the Rosetta-stone sentence, the FAQ answers, and the schema `description` fields. Not a swap — an addition.

### D. Bonus: add "second home" copy
- Sojourn8 leans on "second home" heavily because it's a *massive* keyword in both UK and US.
- Worth a one-line addition on every page: "...a **second home** you co-own rather than buy outright."
