## Quotable founder lines (use for HARO/press)

These are pre-built quotes David can offer to journalists. Each is sharp, specific, and quotable.

1. **On the difference from timeshare:** "The cleanest mental model is: in a timeshare you own time, in fractional ownership you own a slice of real estate. The first depreciates; the second tracks the underlying property value."

2. **On the European fractional market:** "Europe is structurally different from the US fractional market. US operators like Pacaso win on Aspen and Lake Tahoe; Europe is fragmented across MYNE, Vivla, &Hamlet and August Collection — each strong in their own micro-market."

3. **On who actually buys:** "The typical buyer isn't a yield investor. It's a couple who'd happily own a €2 million Mallorca villa outright but resent paying €120k a year in fees and taxes for a home they use eight weeks. Fractional turns that into €300k purchase and €15k a year."

4. **On exits:** "The single most-asked question we get is 'what if I want to sell?' The answer is: yes, you can — but the secondary market is thinner than the primary market and you should expect 3-6 months to find a buyer at fair value."

5. **On tax structuring:** "The LLC wrapper that holds the property is the unsung hero of fractional ownership. It keeps the share tradable, isolates each owner from the others' liabilities, and dramatically simplifies cross-border tax filings."

6. **On growth of the category:** "We listed ~330 properties at the start of 2026. The number of European fractional listings has roughly tripled in two years. This isn't a niche anymore — it's a sub-asset class."

7. **On the US-vs-Europe pricing gap:** "A US 1/8 share averages ~$900k. A European 1/8 share averages ~€220k. Same model, very different underlying property economics."

8. **On what's overhyped:** "Yield projections. Most fractional operators quote net rental yields of 5-10%, but those numbers assume full-week premium occupancy. Realistic net yield after fees is closer to 2-4%."

9. **On what's underrated:** "The booking-rotation algorithm. Whether your fractional purchase is a good or bad experience hinges almost entirely on how the operator allocates peak weeks. It's worth grilling them on this before buying."

10. **On Pacaso vs MYNE:** "Pacaso is the most polished operator in the category but heavily concentrated in US destinations. MYNE has the broadest European footprint but variable photo quality and renovation standards across listings. Different buyers, different fits."
