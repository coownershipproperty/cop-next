About this tracker

This dashboard is the manual layer of the AI visibility tracking system in ai-strategy-master.md §6. It records what AI engines surface for COP-relevant queries over time, so you can see whether the strategy is moving the needle.

Sister documents in /research-output/:

  • ai-strategy-master.md — the master strategy doc (read first)
  • ai-search-research.md — deep engine fact sheets + AEO/GEO evidence grading
  • site-audit.md — full COP technical + content audit
  • baseline.md — initial 26-query competitive baseline (May 26, 2026)
  • manual-prompt-library.md — the ~120-prompt testing library

A future iteration could call AI engine APIs directly (Perplexity Sonar, OpenAI, Brave Search) to automate the logging. For now this is a fast, friction-free way to keep a running record by hand.
