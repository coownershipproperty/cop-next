# Partner Operations Intel v2 — co-ownership-property.com

A neutral, operational reference on the five operator partners whose properties are sold via co-ownership-property.com (COP). Compiled by deep page-by-page reading of each operator's own site, supported by trade press only where the operator's own materials are silent. Every material claim is sourced. Where the operator's site is vague, that is stated explicitly.

Last refreshed: 2026-05-27.

---

## Method + scope

This v2 supersedes the v1 document (`research-output/partner-intel.md`). It differs from v1 in four ways:

1. **Geographies are corrected to match COP's actual inventory** in `lib/properties.json`. v1 claimed Pacaso operates in "40+ destinations," MYNE in "DACH plus 6 other countries," and &Hamlet in "Norway + Spain." None of those are quite right for the COP catalog. The corrected ground-truth counts below come from running the COP partners file on 2026-05-27.
2. **Financing is the load-bearing dimension.** v1 treated financing as one bullet among 18. In v2 it is a structured matrix at the end, broken out by buyer nationality (American / UK / EU / Other) because most COP buyers are American or British and a financing pathway is only useful to them if they're eligible.
3. **Rental policy is simplified.** v1 listed local rental licensing rules in detail per region. v2 treats this as binary at the partner level — Pacaso prohibits owner rentals, all four others permit them — with one universal caveat that local licensing (Mallorca, Paris, parts of Tuscany, etc.) is the rate-limiter, not the operator.
4. **Neutral operational tone throughout.** v1 included controversies, lawsuits, NPR/Vice/Real Deal coverage, financial-loss figures, and community-opposition stories. v2 strips all of that out. This is for COP's downstream comparison content, which will be reviewed by each partner before publishing. Pacaso is a COP partner. Nothing here is framed as critical of any operator.

Every URL accessed for this v2 is listed at the end. Where v1 included a claim that this v2 corrects, the per-partner "What changed from v1" entry calls it out.

## Pacaso

### 1. Company identity
- **Legal entity (operator):** Pacaso Inc., a Delaware C-corporation. Each property is held by a property-specific manager-managed multi-member LLC; the LLC is the deeded owner, and each buyer holds a membership interest in that LLC. (Source: Pacaso Owner FAQ.)
- **Founded:** October 2020.
- **HQ:** San Francisco, California (447 Sutter Street STE 405 PMB 250, San Francisco, CA 94108).
- **Co-founders:** Austin Allison (CEO; previously founder of Dotloop, acquired by Zillow Group in 2015) and Spencer Rascoff (Executive Chairman; former CEO of Zillow, co-founder of Hotwire).
- **California real-estate licensing:** California DRE #02139624.
- **Owner count:** Pacaso states its co-ownership platform has facilitated more than $1.2 billion in transactions; specific 2026 owner count not reiterated in its current public materials.

### 2. Funding
- **Total disclosed equity raised:** Approximately $300M+ cumulative through October 2025.
- **Headline rounds (from press.pacaso.com):**
  - March 2021 Series B: $75M at ~$1B valuation.
  - September 2021 Series C: $125M led by SoftBank Vision Fund 2 at $1.5B valuation.
  - July 2025: $35M raised as initial tranche of an SEC Regulation A+ offering, from ~10,000 individual investors.
  - October 2025: Closes oversubscribed Reg A+ at $72.5M total from 17,500+ individual investors — Pacaso describes this as the largest real-estate Reg A+ raise of 2025.
- **Ticker reserved:** NASDAQ:PCSO (reserved May 2025; no IPO filing as of this report).
- **Profitability (per Pacaso press release of Sep 2025):** $12.6M adjusted gross profit in H1 2025, improving margins, reduced cash burn.

### 3. Geographic coverage
- **Per COP inventory (190 listings):** USA 161 · Mexico 12 · France 7 · England 6 · Italy 3 · Spain 1.
- **US destinations within the 161 US listings:** California (Lake Tahoe, Napa/Sonoma, Palm Springs, Malibu, La Jolla, etc.), Florida (Palm Beach, 30A area), Hawaii, Colorado (Aspen, Breckenridge, Steamboat Springs, Telluride, Vail), the Carolinas (Kiawah Island), Texas, Utah (Park City, Heber City).
- **International destinations on COP:** Paris and Côte d'Azur (France); London and the Cotswolds area (England); Cabo San Lucas, San Jose del Cabo, Punta Mita (Mexico); Florence, Milan, Rome (Italy); Marbella (Spain).
- **Operator-marketed expansion not yet in COP catalog:** Pacaso announced Caribbean entry in July 2025 (Anguilla, Bahamas, British Virgin Islands, Cayman Islands, St. Barts) — these are not in COP's current inventory.
- **v1 claim "40+ destinations" — that figure is Pacaso's own marketing language and refers to US municipal markets. The country footprint represented in the COP catalog is 6 countries.**

### 4. Property profile
- **Positioning:** Single-family luxury second homes in the US; apartments/penthouses in the European cultural capitals (London, Paris, Florence, Milan, Rome) and Mexican beach destinations.
- **Typical 1/8 share price (from current COP listings):** roughly $500k–$1.4M+. Sample current 1/8 share prices include Magnolia (Kiawah Island, SC) $678k; Sky Valley (Heber City, UT) $1.035M; Charles Street (London) $1.395M; Casa Bianca (Tuscany) $549k; Costa Azul (Cabo San Lucas) $717k.
- **Curation philosophy:** Pacaso buys each home directly, performs light renovation/upgrade, professionally furnishes it (interior designers credited in Architectural Digest and House Beautiful per Pacaso copy), then resells it as 8 shares. The LLC is named on the deed; buyers receive LLC membership interests.

### 5. Share structure
- **Default share size:** 1/8.
- **Larger share sizes available:** 1/4, 1/2. Pacaso's own materials reference "1/8 to 1/2."
- **Owners per property:** Up to 8 (with multi-share owners reducing the headcount accordingly).
- **Days/year per 1/8:** Pacaso states owners enjoy their home "an average of 6–7 times during the year" (coownership page). Operator does not publish a fixed days/year cap on the public site beyond the SmartStay scheduling rules below — v1's "44 nights" figure was not verified verbatim on the current public site.

### 6. Legal structure
- **US:** Property-specific manager-managed multi-member LLC. Pacaso quote: "We use a multi-member manager-managed LLC. These LLCs are a unified front that represent and protect a group of buyers. Each LLC has its own bank account, tax ID number and holds title to the Pacaso Home." (Owner FAQ.)
- **France:** Pacaso's France blog and intl-ownership page reference SCI (Société Civile Immobilière) as the canonical French civil-law property holding structure, but Pacaso does not publish a SCI-specific shareholder agreement on its public site.
- **Italy, Spain, UK:** Operator's site does not specify the exact entity per country in public materials; describes itself as "partnering with local legal experts" and using "local equivalent" structures. **Operator's site does not specify per-country entity types.**
- **Voting / governance:** Each share holds one vote. Material changes to the operating agreement require an ownership vote. Non-material changes (e.g., state-reporting address updates) Pacaso can make on its own. Owners can vote to remove Pacaso as program manager and self-manage the home (Pacaso FAQ).

### 7. Pricing transparency
- Listing pages on pacaso.com show the 1/8 (or 1/4) share price. Whole-home equivalent is not stated explicitly but can be computed (8× share price).
- **One-time service fee:** Pacaso markets a "12% service fee" on initial primary purchase per its own coownership/business materials (verified via multiple secondary press sources; not stated verbatim on the coownership page at the time of this report). **Operator's current public coownership page does not state the 12% figure inline — confirm with David / Pacaso sales rep.**
- **Annual operating estimate:** Pacaso publishes the existence of a Home Operating Estimate (FAQ) but not a specific % range. "Pacaso Accounting reviews the Home Operating Estimate on a quarterly basis…Quarterly true-ups or one-time assessments may be needed to reconcile any overages or underages."
- **Technology Systems Fee:** Pacaso charges a separate Technology Systems Fee for access to its SmartStay app and platform; the specific dollar amount is not stated on the current public page (v1 referenced $99/month — that figure is not currently visible on Pacaso's public Owner FAQ).

### 8. Annual fees model
- **Model:** Cost-pass-through plus a Technology Systems Fee. Home Operating Estimate is "based on the previous year actuals and takes into account market-level and macroeconomic changes, as well as projected maintenance and capital projects" (Owner FAQ).
- **Reconciliation:** "Quarterly true-ups or one-time assessments may be needed to reconcile any overages or underages" (Owner FAQ).
- **Coverage:** Property management, maintenance, utilities, taxes, insurance, reserve fund. Reserve fund "held in the individual LLC's bank account, managed by Pacaso. Funds are only used for the home's repairs and maintenance" (Owner FAQ).
- **Owners can vote** to use reserve funds for upgrades/replacements beyond routine items.

### 9. Resale process
- **Owner-set price:** "Pacaso recommends a market price, but the seller makes the final call" (Owner FAQ).
- **12-month hold for new homes:** "Applies to homes where all ownership interests have been sold. If your Pacaso has available interests, you can sell your unit after 12 months of ownership" (Owner FAQ).
- **Marketing channels for resale:** Pacaso markets to existing owners, its marketplace prospect pool, then open market (own site + Pacaso-promoted channels + partner agent network).
- **Seller commission:** "For the seller, a standard commission fee is deducted from the final sale price" — specific % not published on the current page. v1 referenced 6%; **not currently visible verbatim on the Pacaso public site — confirm with David / Pacaso sales rep**.
- **Buyer transaction costs:** "For the buyer, there are no title, inspection or service fees on resale transactions. Buyers make a small non-refundable contribution to the reserve account…Buyers who choose to finance their purchase also pay a financing fee at closing" (Owner FAQ).
- **Average resale time / appreciation:** Pacaso's coownership page states "Pacaso homes have an average historical increase of nearly 10% upon resale — that's twice the appreciation for a traditional luxury vacation home." Specific average days-on-market not on current page; v1's "99 days" figure was not re-verified.
- **Forced-sale fallback:** "Pacaso can sell the entire property in the unlikely event that more than three shares are in default for more than 90 days, and the shares fail to sell for the loan-to-value ratio of 50%. Before a property can be sold, each owner will have the right to purchase the defaulted shares at a discount" (Owner FAQ).

### 10. Mortgage / financing — by buyer nationality
**This is the load-bearing dimension; see Matrix B for the cross-partner view.**

- **Pacaso financing page** (pacaso.com/financing): "Together with our banking partners, Pacaso offers competitive financing for up to 70% of the home's purchase price."
- **Minimum down payment:** 30%.
- **Loan structure:** "The loan is between our banking partner and the LLC. Borrowers access financing directly from the LLC" (Financing FAQ).
- **Default protection:** "Pacaso serves as the corporate guarantor of any share financing, protecting you should another owner default…If not resolved within 90 days, we will foreclose on that specific share and manage the resale" (Owner FAQ + Financing page).
- **Payment options:** Cash, crypto (via BitPay), HELOC / personal line of credit, or Pacaso-arranged financing up to 70% LTV.
- **Eligibility — non-US buyers:** Pacaso's financing page does not explicitly extend the 70% LTV offer to non-US buyers. Third-party press references state Pacaso's integrated financing is "for qualifying U.S. buyers." Pacaso's lender-partner program appears to be US-centric. **For UK/EU buyers of Pacaso US homes, financing is most likely to be sourced separately via the buyer's own international mortgage broker — confirm with Pacaso sales rep before publishing.**
- **Eligibility — for buyers purchasing Pacaso European homes (UK, France, Italy):** Operator site does not document whether Pacaso's financing program extends to European-property purchases. Pacaso's intl-ownership page says Pacaso "leverages local expertise to simplify buying, financing and owning a home abroad" but does not name local lender partners.

### 11. Cooling-off period
- **Not specifically advertised** on the current Pacaso public site as a cooling-off period.
- In the US, real-estate purchase contracts are governed by state law and (for the Reg A+ securities offering by Pacaso itself, separately) SEC rules. Pacaso's coownership product is real-estate, not securities, so the standard federal 3-day cooling-off rule (which applies to door-to-door consumer sales) doesn't apply. Some state-specific statutory rescission may apply per state.
- Pacaso's UK and EU contracts may differ; **operator's site does not specify a cooling-off period.**

### 12. Rental program
- **Pacaso prohibits owner rentals.** Pacaso homes are owner-and-guest-only.
- **Implication:** A Pacaso share generates lifestyle and potential capital-appreciation value, but **no ongoing rental income**. This is the single biggest operational difference vs the other four partners.
- **Local-licensing caveat:** Not applicable to Pacaso since rentals are prohibited at the operator level regardless of local rules.

### 13. Scheduling / calendar
- **SmartStay™ scheduling system** in the Pacaso owner app.
- **Two stay types** (coownership page FAQ):
  - **Advance stays:** Bookable 8 days to 24 months in advance. "Each owner has access to an advance stay that falls on a special date (e.g., federal holidays and popular local events)."
  - **Short-notice stays:** Bookable 2 to 30 days in advance. (For homes where some shares haven't yet been sold, the short-notice window narrows to 2–7 days.)
- **Network swap:** Pacaso owners can swap stays with owners of other Pacaso homes worldwide (operator describes this as "Swap" on the coownership page).
- **Max consecutive stay:** Not stated on current public page — v1 referenced 14 nights but that figure was not verbatim verified on current pages.

### 14. Concierge / management scope

- Pre-arrival inspection, linens, towels, paper goods, kitchen essentials restocked.
- Cleaning, turnover, vendor management, recurring + preventive maintenance, financial oversight all included.
- Among the most operationally heavy / full-service of the 5 COP partners. Pacaso describes itself as "fully managed LLC co-ownership."

- **Industry / financial coverage:** Press releases distributed on press.pacaso.com cover the Reg A+ closing, Italy launch, Caribbean launch, half-year 2025 results.

### 5. Share structure

### 6. Legal structure

### 7. Pricing transparency

### 8. Annual fees model
- **3-bucket structure** (per Spain FAQ blog post, verbatim):
  1. **Consumption-based costs** (paid according to usage): Electricity, water, heating, cleaning, laundry.
  2. **Fixed costs** (paid according to number of shares): Insurance, maintenance and upkeep (pool, garden), maintenance reserve for property and inventory, administrative costs for the property-holding company (bookkeeping).
  3. **MYNE Service Fee:** Professional and value-orientated management; co-owner coordination; external service-provider coordination; MYNE premium owner service.
- **Budget approved per property** by co-owners, reconciled year-end.
- **% of fraction value:** Not published. **Need to confirm via brochure or direct ask to MYNE — see Open Questions.**

### 9. Resale process
- **Operator quote** (on how-myne-works page): "As a co-owner, you can easily sell your share at market value."
- **MYNE markets the share** to existing prospect pool; new buyer is vetted for lifestyle fit.
- **Resale commission / timeline:** Not publicly published. v1 noted this gap; still a gap in v2.
- **Liquidity:** "Seamless resale processes" referenced as a benefit of the GmbH & Co. KG structure but no specific average days-on-market or appreciation figure published.

### 10. Mortgage / financing — by buyer nationality
**This is the load-bearing dimension; see Matrix B for the cross-partner view.**

- **MYNE financing blog post** (myne-homes.com/blog/how-to-finance-co-ownership) lays out 4 options:
  1. **Equity** — pay your share in cash.
  2. **Bank financing** — "a share of the property is financed through a mortgage, much like a traditional property purchase. Depending on the bank and financing model, co-owners can obtain financing to acquire their share. Many banks now offer specialised financing products for fractional or co-ownership models."
  3. **Third-party financing** — independent advisors and specialised providers.
  4. **MYNE "Buy now, pay later" model** — "MYNE offer flexible financing models that allow for a smaller initial investment…a share can be acquired with the option to pay the full purchase price at a later date but interest has to be paid for the payment plan."
- **MYNE does not name an exclusive lender partner** in public materials. Mortgages are case-by-case via the buyer's own bank.
- **MYNE encumbrance rules:** Other co-owners must accept the credit terms of any individual financing but are not joint borrowers. Only the buyer's own share can be encumbered.
- **Eligibility — non-DACH buyers:** Not directly stated. The model is recognized across the EU per the legal-structure blog. **For UK and US buyers, financing must be arranged via the buyer's own international-mortgage broker — MYNE does not appear to broker financing directly. Confirm with David / MYNE sales rep before publishing.**
- **MYNE's "Buy now, pay later" is the most internally-controlled financing option** — it's offered by MYNE itself rather than by a partner bank, so eligibility likely doesn't depend on buyer nationality. **Confirm.**
