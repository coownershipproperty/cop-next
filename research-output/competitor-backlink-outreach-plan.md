**Date:** 3 June 2026
**Backlink task #8 — reverse competitor backlink mining + outreach**
**Status:** Methodology + email templates ready. Needs Ahrefs Webmaster access (free) to pull the target list.

## The play in one sentence

Find every site that links to Pacaso, MYNE Homes, Vivla, &Hamlet, or August Collection. Email those site owners offering COP as the *neutral marketplace overview* that complements their existing coverage. Realistic: 50 emails/week → 1 link/week → ~50 high-relevance links per year.

## Why it works

Sites that have already written about a fractional ownership operator have proven they cover the category. They're:
1. **Topically relevant** — already linking to fractional content
2. **Receptive to comparisons** — bloggers love neutral category overviews because they don't have to be the comparison authority themselves
3. **Active link maintainers** — they update articles when they learn of new resources

The pitch isn't "link to us" — it's "you wrote about Pacaso; here's the neutral marketplace that aggregates Pacaso alongside MYNE, Vivla, &Hamlet and others. Your readers comparing options would value this."

## AI-drafting workflow (the part that makes this scale)

For each of the 100-500 prospects:

1. Pull the article URL + their email (free from Hunter.io / Apollo / RocketReach trial)
2. AI-summarise the article in 2 sentences → context for the email
3. AI-draft Variant A using their name + their article quote
4. David reviews + sends the top 10% personalised, AI-sends the rest as-is

**Realistic effort:** 1 hour per 25 emails (drafting + sending). 50 emails/week = 2 hours.

## Conversion math (honest expectations)

- 50 emails sent per week
- 30-40% open rate (cold but topically relevant)
- 5-10% reply rate
- 2-3% conversion to link added

**= 1 link per week ≈ 50 high-relevance links per year**

50 high-relevance links from sites that already write about fractional ownership = real SEO + AI authority signal. Compounded over 12 months it's transformative.
