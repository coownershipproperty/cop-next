## Why this is still the single highest-leverage backlink play in 2026

AI engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini) cite Wikipedia disproportionately when answering questions. A single legitimate citation on the "Fractional ownership" or "Timeshare" article gets COP surfaced in AI answers for years.

The revised plan focuses on **adding COP as a citation to existing Wikipedia articles** — not on creating Wikipedia articles about other operators (which David has confirmed is not something we want to do).

## Wikipedia editing ethics — how to do this properly

**Never write your own company's Wikipedia article** — Wikipedia auto-flags self-promotional edits and reverts them. The technique:

1. **Spend 2-3 weeks building an editor account reputation** — make 30-40 unrelated edits (typo fixes, formatting cleanup, expanding poorly-sourced sections) on completely unrelated articles.
2. **Add COP citations to EXISTING articles where there's a genuine citation gap** — don't promote, just source claims that need sources.
3. **Use the talk page** to propose substantial additions before making them — gets community buy-in.
