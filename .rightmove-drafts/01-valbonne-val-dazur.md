> Villa Val d'Azur is a four-bed fractional villa set within a private gated domain, just minutes from the historic village of Valbonne on the French Riviera. Approximately 107m² of bright Provençal living opens onto a landscaped garden with mature olive trees, a private swimming pool and a covered barbecue area, with glimpses of the Mediterranean Sea and the Alps in the distance.
>
> The interior offers three bathrooms, a fireplace and well-proportioned reception spaces, all currently undergoing a curated turnkey upgrade: fresh interior paint, new furniture and a warm, elegant style — designed so owners arrive ready to enjoy from day one. Air conditioning, secure parking and high-speed internet are included.
>
> Within the gated domain itself, residents have access to private tennis courts, beach volleyball, a communal pool and beautifully kept green areas. Valbonne — seven minutes away — offers authentic Provençal life with cafés, weekly markets and a vibrant village square, while Nice, Antibes and Cannes are within easy reach. The region enjoys over 300 days of sunshine, with the Riviera beaches just around the corner and the Alps for winter skiing. Ownership is structured as 1/8 fractional with a real deeded title — this is not a timeshare or a rental club.
