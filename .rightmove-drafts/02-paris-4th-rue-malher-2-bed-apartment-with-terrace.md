## Tab 4 — Full description (~1247 chars)

> Set in an 18th-century building on Rue Malher in the heart of Le Marais — one block from the legendary Rue des Rosiers and a 5-minute walk to Place des Vosges — this two-bed fractional apartment offers something increasingly rare in central Paris: a 10-square-metre private terrace.
> 
> The layout thoughtfully separates the two bedrooms with the living spaces between them, giving natural privacy for two couples travelling together or a family seeking separation. The guest bedroom is highly versatile: two daybeds with trundles can be configured before your arrival as two queens, four singles, or any combination needed.
> 
> Note on the share structure: this is a fractional ownership offered through Paris Property Group, structured differently from a standard 1/8 share. The €237,000 share buys 4 weeks of personal use per year — fixed weeks 24 and 25 (beginning June 6) plus two floating weeks. Annual dues are approximately €5,000, remarkably low for a luxury Paris fractional. The ownership community has been in place for over 15 years.
> 
> The apartment is turnkey and beautifully renovated, on the 1st floor (2nd American) of a historic building. No elevator. Within easy walking distance of the Marais galleries, bistros, and Place des Vosges.
